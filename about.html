About Our Club

Founded to bring together gamers across the institute, our club provides a structured environment for both casual enthusiasts and hardcore competitors.

Mission & Vision

Mission: To promote teamwork, strategic thinking, and sportsmanship through organized competitive gaming.

Vision: To establish our campus as a premier powerhouse in university esports leagues across the region.

Our Objectives

  • Organize competitive leagues for popular mobile, PC, and console titles.
  • Provide a platform for members to improve their mechanics, communication, and game sense.
  • Host regular stream watch-parties, strategy workshops, and social LAN events.
